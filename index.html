Welcome home.

Two drinks. Same pitcher. Pick the one that sounds better. No caffeine in either.

Pitcher already in the fridge? Skip to the glasses. If it isn’t, mix the base first. It makes about 4 drinks.

The base

Stir these together in the glass pitcher.

Caramel vodka1 cup
Apple cider1 ½ cups
Fresh lemon juice¼ cup
Ground cinnamon½ tsp

Pick a glass

Ice in the glass, then this. One drink each.

Quieter

Caramel Orchard

Short glass. Maple and bitters. No bubbles.

  1. Fill a short glass with ice.
  2. Pour in 5 oz of the pitcher. A generous short pour, a little over ½ cup.
  3. Add ½ tablespoon maple syrup.
  4. Add 1 dash of bitters.
  5. Stir. Apple slice on the rim if you feel like it.
Fizzy

Caramel Apple

Taller glass. Ginger ale. The brighter one.

  1. Fill a taller glass with ice.
  2. Pour in 5 oz of the pitcher.
  3. Top with 2 oz ginger ale. About 4 tablespoons.
  4. Stir once so it stays fizzy.
  5. Apple slice if you want it to look like the picture.

You made it through Friday. That’s the drink.
